Programmes

Four programmes. Three awarding bodies. One campus in Dhanmondi.

Choose the route that matches your results. Entry requirements, assessment and the next step are set out for each programme. Where the Academy has not published a detail, we say so and ask you to contact the office.

01

University of London · Level 4

CertHE in Common Law

For applicants without A Levels.

Awarding body
University of London
Duration
One year, the first year of the University of London LLB (120 credits)
Assessment
University of London examinations. Final examinations are conducted by the British Council, Dhaka, and marked and certified by the University of London.
Entry
Any one of the three routes below.

Entry requirements

  • Five O Levels with at least four C grades.
  • SSC and HSC with one 1st division and one 2nd division (old system).
  • A total GPA of 5 or more (minimum 2 SSC + 2 HSC + 1).

The Academy may require a written English test and a viva.

The CertHE can lead to the University of London LLB, subject to progression. Its four modules are the first-year modules listed under the LL.B (Hons) below. The Academy was the first institution in Bangladesh permitted by the University of London to run this programme, which was earlier the Diploma in Law.

Ask a student counsellor to check your results against these routes. Send an inquiry.

02

University of London · Levels 4 to 6

LL.B (Hons)

The classic route to a UK law degree from Dhaka.

Awarding body
University of London
Duration
3 years
Assessment
University of London examinations. Final examinations are conducted by the British Council, Dhaka, and marked and certified by the University of London.
Entry
University of London General Entrance Requirements, plus one of the two routes below.

Entry requirements

  • Meet the University of London General Entrance Requirements for international students, and
  • (a) a minimum C in 3 O Levels and 2 A Levels, or
  • (b) a minimum C in 1 O Level and 3 A Levels.

You apply to the University of London for registration through the Academy.

Admissions are open for the October 2027 assessment cycle. Ask us for the registration steps.

University of Hertfordshire · Levels 4, 5 and 6

LLB (Hons) SDL and LLM (SDL)

A UK university degree with a route into Bar Training at Hertfordshire.

Awarding body
University of Hertfordshire
Duration
Ask us
Assessment
Ask us
Entry
Ask us

Supported Distance Learning (SDL) means you study the University of Hertfordshire degree with support at the Academy in Dhaka. After the LLB (Hons) SDL, students can continue to the University of Hertfordshire Bar Training Course in the UK. In 2026 a graduate of this route progressed to Bar training at Hertfordshire.

The Academy also lists the University of Hertfordshire LLM (SDL). Ask us for its details.

The January 2027 intake is open for Levels 4, 5 and 6. Ask a counsellor how to apply.
