The pathway

From a first year to the Bar.

The route most law students take, in five steps. Your own path depends on your results and the programme you choose.

  1. Year 1

    CertHE in Common Law

    University of London, Level 4. The entry point for applicants without A Levels.

  2. Years 2 and 3

    LL.B (Hons)

    University of London, taught at the Academy, with examinations at the British Council, Dhaka.

  3. Alongside, from January 2027

    LLB (Hons) SDL

    University of Hertfordshire, Levels 4, 5 and 6, an alternative route in Dhaka.

  4. After the LLB

    Bar Training Course

    University of Hertfordshire, in the UK, after the LLB.

  5. Then

    Called to the Bar

    The Pupillage Advisory Service at the Academy helps with CVs, chambers and mock interviews.

The Academy in four facts

Thirty-seven years, and the record to show for it.

  • 1989Founded in Dhaka by the late Barrister A.K.M. Mozammel Hoque Bhuiyan.
  • 600+Graduates, barristers and business graduates. Among the alumni are current Justices of the Supreme Court of Bangladesh, MPs and government officials.
  • FirstInstitution in Bangladesh permitted by the University of London to run the CertHE Common Law.
  • UoLUniversity of London Recognised Teaching Centre.
