Privacy

Preview only.

Preview only. The live site will carry the Academy's own policy.

This demonstration site does not store personal data and has no database. The inquiry form checks your entries in your browser and stops there. The registration form opens WhatsApp or your email app with the details you typed already written out; nothing is sent until you press Send, and it goes to the Academy, not to this site. While you answer, your answers are kept in your own browser tab so a refresh does not lose them, and they are cleared when you close the tab.
