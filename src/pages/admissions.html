How to apply

Four steps.

  1. Step 1Talk to a counsellor. Register online, message us on WhatsApp, call the office or send the inquiry form below.
  2. Step 2Check the entry requirements. The CertHE and the University of London LL.B have different routes. Both are listed on the programmes page.
  3. Step 3Register with the awarding body. The Academy helps you register with the University of London or the University of Hertfordshire.
  4. Step 4Join classes. Ask us for the start dates and your timetable.
