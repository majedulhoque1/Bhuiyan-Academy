## CertHE/LLB 1st Year (4 Modules, 120 Credits)
- Contract law [LA1040]
- Criminal law [LA1010]
- Legal system and method [LA1031]
- Public law [LA1020]

## LLB 2nd Year (4 Modules, 120 Credits)
- Tort law [LA2001]
- Administrative law [LA2008]
- EU law [LA2024]
- Property law [LA2003]

## LLB 3rd Year (4 Modules, 120 Credits)
- Jurisprudence and legal theory [LA3005]
- Equity and Trusts [LA3002]
- "And Two more modules, chosen from:"
  - Company law [LA3021]
  - Dissertation [LA3200]
  - Introduction to Islamic law [LA3028]

Note: credits are stated per year (120 per year), not per module. Year 3 lists two fixed modules plus two chosen from the three options.
