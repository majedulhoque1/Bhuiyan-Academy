Register for a programme.

Three quick questions, about a minute. Your answers are written out for you in WhatsApp and you press Send. A student counsellor then checks your results and calls you.

What happens next

Register, then we call.

  1. Step 1Answer three questions. Programme, your highest qualification, and how to reach you.
  2. Step 2Press Send in WhatsApp. Your answers are already written there.
  3. Step 3A counsellor calls you. They check your results, explain the fees and give you the start date.

Registering sends a request, not a confirmed place. Your place is confirmed after the counsellor has checked your results and you have registered with the awarding body.
