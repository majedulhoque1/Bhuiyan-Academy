Why we looked

Kiron studied here.

Anisul Islam Kiron is a former student of the Academy and now works with us at EXPERIUS, a Dhaka team that builds websites and business systems. When he showed us the Academy's site, we thought the Academy deserved better than what a visitor sees today. So we did not write a proposal first. We built the preview and want you to judge it.

Nothing here is published, and nothing was sent anywhere. Every student in the portal is fictional.

What we saw

Three gaps, stated with respect.

The Academy's record is strong: since 1989, a University of London Recognised Teaching Centre, 600+ graduates. The website does not yet carry that weight, especially on a phone, which is how most applicants and parents will first visit.

  • PhonesThe current pages are laid out for a desktop screen. On a phone the menu and the banner are cut off, as the picture below shows.
  • ApplyingThere is no admission form. A visitor has to find a phone number or chat link and write to the office, with nothing recorded on the Academy's side.
  • StudentsEnrolled students have no single place for notices, timetable, results and fee statements.

What we built

A website, written from the Academy's own facts.

Programmes, entry requirements, facilities, faculty, scholarships and contact details are taken from the Academy's own pages. Where the Academy has not published something, such as fees, the preview says "Ask us" and does not guess.

  • HomeA UK law degree, taught in Dhaka since 1989, with the route from first year to the Bar drawn as you scroll. Open it.
  • ProgrammesFour programmes, with awarding body, entry route and the University of London modules. Open it.
  • AdmissionsThe next intake, how to apply, and an inquiry form. Open it.
  • About, ContactThe Academy's history, leadership, faculty, facilities, and every phone number in one place. About · Contact.

The student portal

One place for notices, timetable, results and fees.

This is a clickable demonstration with sample data. It shows what students and the office would see. It takes about a minute to try.

  1. 1Open the portal and choose Enter office view.
  2. 2Under Post a notice, write a title and a line of text, then post it.
  3. 3Switch to Student and open Notices. The notice is already on the board.

Two of your posts, redesigned

Same facts. Easier to read on a phone.

We took two recent posts from the Academy's Facebook page and redesigned them. The wording and facts are the Academy's. The first one now uses the Academy's WhatsApp number 01755557252 as the only contact, and leaves fee questions for a counsellor.

What a portal does for the Academy

Fewer calls. One record.

We do not promise numbers. We describe what the system does, and the Academy can judge whether it matters.

  • NoticesThe office posts once. Every student sees it, instead of calls, messages and a Facebook post.
  • FeesEach student sees instalments, due dates and what has been paid. The office sees who has a balance.
  • AdmissionsInquiries from the website arrive in one place with the programme the person asked about.
  • RecordsStudents, batches, results and resources sit in one system that the Academy owns, not in separate files.

What happens next

A conversation first. A written proposal second.

If the preview is useful, the next step is a 25-minute call with the Principal or the office. We walk through it, and you tell us what we got wrong. After that we send a written, scoped proposal with a fixed price. There is no obligation, and the preview stays available while you decide.

  1. 1Look at the preview and the portal. Tell us what is wrong or missing.
  2. 2A 25-minute call, at the Academy or by phone.
  3. 3A written proposal with scope, timeline and price. We reply within two working days of the call.
