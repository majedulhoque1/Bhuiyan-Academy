About the Academy

Thirty-seven years of preparing lawyers and managers.

A University of London Recognised Teaching Centre in Dhanmondi, with University of Hertfordshire and National University programmes alongside.

Heritage

From a founder's idea in 1989 to 600+ graduates.

  1. 1989

    The Academy is founded

    Barrister A.K.M. Mozammel Hoque Bhuiyan opens the Academy in Dhaka.

  2. First

    First in Bangladesh to run the CertHE Common Law

    The Academy was the first institution in Bangladesh permitted by the University of London to run this programme, earlier the Diploma in Law.

  3. 2006-07

    International moot court, Pune

    Academy students take part in the International Moot Court Competition in Pune, India.

  4. 2023

    University of Hertfordshire LLB SDL launches

    The Academy and the University of Hertfordshire launch the LLB (Hons) Supported Distance Learning programme on 17 August 2023.

  5. Today

    600+ graduates

    Barristers and business graduates. Among the alumni are current Justices of the Supreme Court of Bangladesh, MPs and government officials.

The founder

Barrister A.K.M. Mozammel Hoque Bhuiyan

1939 to 2008

The late Barrister A.K.M. Mozammel Hoque Bhuiyan founded the Academy in 1989. He was called to the Bar in July 1973, qualified as FCA in 1986 and held an M.Com from Dhaka University. He taught Fiscal Law part-time at Dhaka University and served as honorary Chairman of the CRP Trustee Board from 1982 to 2004.

Around 300 students became barristers through the Academy's LLB, by the Academy's own count. In all, more than 600 graduates have come through its programmes.

Messages

In the Academy's own words.

Principal, Dr Rabia Bhuiyan · Adapted from the current site

Bhuiyan Academy is a private institution of higher education offering distance learning programmes. Our programmes for external degrees run closely with the awarding universities and colleges, and attract students from all over Bangladesh.

Since 1989 the Academy has been committed to producing competent professionals who are academically prepared for the challenges of today's society. Teachers and students work together, and that has led to impressive examination results. Our teaching staff are specialists in their own fields and encourage students to take part and ask questions in class. By keeping students' needs first, students and the Academy benefit together.

Faculty of Law · Adapted from the current site

The Faculty of Law began in 1989, founded by the late Barrister A.K.M. Mozammel Hoque Bhuiyan. The Academy was the first institution in Bangladesh permitted to conduct the University of London Diploma in Law, and it opened a route to foreign legal education and to becoming a Barrister-at-Law for a great many Bangladeshi students.

Our faculty is full-time and part-time teachers who engage in research, seminars and article writing. The moot court builds advocacy skills, and the Pupillage Advisory Service gives advice on CVs, chambers and mock interviews.
